# Sky imagery credits

Both images are NASA material, in the public domain.

- `sun-hot.jpg`: SDO/AIA 304 Å (the chromosphere) and `sun-gold.jpg`:
  SDO/AIA 171 Å (the corona), NASA Solar Dynamics Observatory, quick-look
  images of 2026-09-21, 512 px, whole frame. Offline stand-ins: when online
  the app shows the latest ones from https://sdo.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/
- `moon.jpg`: frame 778 (2026-02-02 09:00 UTC, full moon) of "Moon Phase
  and Libration, 2026", NASA Scientific Visualization Studio (visualizer
  Ernie Wright, USRA), built from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter data. Cropped
  to the disc, 512 px. Offline stand-in: when online the app shows the frame
  for the current hour from the same set. https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5587/
